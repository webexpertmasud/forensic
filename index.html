02 / About us

Science that speaks to real-world questions.

Forensic Neuroscience Training Academy brings together neuroscience, behavioural science, and the justice system through accessible education and thoughtful professional support.

Our mission is to help people understand what brain science can—and cannot—tell us, translating complex research into clear insights for practice, policy, and everyday learning.
